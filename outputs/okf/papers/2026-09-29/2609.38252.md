### Abstract (原文)
> With the proliferation of free online editing tools, altering or forging pdf documents has become trivially easy, often leaving no visual traces on screen. This paper introduces a two- stage forensic pipeline. An initial security-gating layer validates format compliance and flags embedded malicious payloads and a forensic engine that inspects internal objects across meta- data, visual overlays, and dual-source OCR consistency, etc... A weighted scoring engine aggregates these forensic indicators into an interpretable risk score is proposed. The approach was validated on real medical work-stoppage certificates.
